Ad, tracker and annoyance blocking rules are downloaded when the app runs;
none of them is part of the app. The default lists are EasyList, EasyPrivacy
and Fanboy's Annoyance List (which includes the EasyList Cookie List), all
from https://easylist.to/ and copyright the EasyList authors, dual licensed
under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported license and
the GNU General Public License v3 or later (see
https://easylist.to/pages/licence.html), and uBlock Origin's cookie notice
and annoyance lists from https://github.com/uBlockOrigin/uAssets, GNU General
Public License v3.

Scriptlets and redirect resources are likewise downloaded: the uBlock Origin
resources (GNU General Public License v3, https://github.com/gorhill/uBlock)
as packaged and published by Ghostery (https://github.com/ghostery/adblocker).
They are data the blocking engine runs inside the pages being saved, and are
not linked into the app.
