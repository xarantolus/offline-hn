Ad, tracker and annoyance blocking rules are downloaded when the app runs;
none of them is part of the app. The default lists, each under the terms its
maintainers state:

- EasyList, EasyPrivacy, Fanboy's Annoyance List (which includes the EasyList
  Cookie List), Fanboy's Newsletter List and Adblock Warning Removal, from
  https://easylist.to/, copyright the EasyList authors, under the Creative
  Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported license and the GNU General
  Public License v3 or later (https://easylist.to/pages/licence.html).
- uBlock Origin's filters, privacy, badware, unbreak, quick fix, cookie
  notice and annoyance lists, from https://github.com/uBlockOrigin/uAssets,
  GNU General Public License v3.
- AdGuard's mobile ads, cookie notice, popup, mobile app banner, annoyance,
  widget and social media filters, from
  https://github.com/AdguardTeam/AdguardFilters, GNU General Public License v3.
- Peter Lowe's ad server list, from https://pgl.yoyo.org/adservers/.
- yokoffing's Annoyance List, from https://github.com/yokoffing/filterlists.
- Dandelion Sprout's Browse websites without logging in, from
  https://github.com/DandelionSprout/adfilt.

Scriptlets and redirect resources are likewise downloaded: the uBlock Origin
resources (GNU General Public License v3, https://github.com/gorhill/uBlock)
as packaged and published by Ghostery (https://github.com/ghostery/adblocker).
They are data the blocking engine runs inside the pages being saved, and are
not linked into the app.
